Deterministic shortest-time bounds on a toy graph, in minutes to the fixed destination Clinic, for the 6 nodes A, B, C, D, E and Clinic. Panel (a): original assumptions. Panel (b): widened assumptions, same axes. Each horizontal line is one node's interval between its lower and upper travel time. A filled circle with a solid line means definite access, a filled square with a dashed line means definite exclusion, and a hollow diamond with a dash-dot line means unresolved. The dotted vertical line is the 10-minute access threshold; equality counts as access. No finite upper time exists for D in either panel; that line runs to the right edge without an end marker. No path exists for E in either panel, so nothing is drawn on that row; the decision is definite exclusion. Original: access for A, exclusion for B and E, unresolved for C and D. Widened: unresolved now also for A and B; a definite decision remains only for E and Clinic. The bounds are deterministic assumptions, not confidence intervals; no geography or observed travel time is involved. Exact endpoints: README.md and bounds.csv beside this figure.
