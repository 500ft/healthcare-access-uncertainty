Deterministic shortest-time bounds on a toy graph, in minutes to the fixed destination Clinic, for the 6 nodes A, B, C, D, E and Clinic. Panel (a): original assumptions. Panel (b): widened assumptions, same axes. Each horizontal line is one node's interval between its lower and upper travel time. Colour and fill give the decision, which is also written at the right of each row: a filled blue circle means definite access, a filled orange square means definite exclusion, and a hollow dark grey diamond means unresolved. The dotted grey vertical line is the 10-minute access threshold; equality counts as access. No finite upper time exists for D in either panel; that line runs to the right edge without an end marker. No path exists for E in either panel, so that row carries only the words no path; the decision is definite exclusion. Original: access for A, exclusion for B and E, unresolved for C and D. Widened: unresolved now also for A and B; a definite decision remains only for E and Clinic. The bounds are deterministic assumptions, not confidence intervals; no geography or observed travel time is involved. Exact endpoints: README.md and bounds.csv beside this figure.
