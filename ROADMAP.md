# Roadmap

## Direction and current step

The owner adopted healthcare-access decision stability as the software question
in this repository. The [decision record](docs/ACCESS_STABILITY.md#owner-decision)
limits that adoption to software work. No separate route-planning repository is
needed.

The [toy graph result](evidence/access-stability-20261006/result.json) is executed.
Metadata-only qualification of Idai development inputs and the reserved WFP
candidate is [recorded and hashed](evidence/access-stability-20261006/qualification.json).
The current step is blocked on pinned, licensed historical development inputs
and adequate metadata for independent evaluation. No real-area access result
or observed accuracy is available.

## Finish line

Reproduce a published development access baseline using qualified inputs.
Determine which access decisions survive at least two independently sourced
bound families. Compare observation selection with random, road-class/length
and centrality policies at equal cost. Report resolution and observed error on
independent, preregistered withheld observations as bounds widen.

## Work remaining

- Obtain the development input versions, bytes, licenses and checksums required
  by the [qualification record](evidence/access-stability-20261006/qualification.json).
- Qualify independent bound sources, including common weather effects, missing
  connections and facility availability. Toy intervals do not satisfy this step.
- Resolve candidate observation lineage, dates, mode and incomplete reporting.
  Freeze outcome bytes through a custodian before viewing rows; freeze matching,
  exclusions, costs and metrics before tuning or evaluation. If independence
  cannot be established, seek a separate event or source.
- Execute one development replication, then registered comparisons. Keep modeled
  flood closures separate from observed constraints. Never count unreported
  roads as passable negatives.

## Detector disposition and inspection rules

The detector question is inactive and superseded by the software pivot. Its
scientific outcome remains unresolved. The [history index](docs/history/README.md)
retains the baseline packet, frozen settings, source code and dated reports.
Keep candidate layers hidden and the holdout unopened. This pivot authorizes
neither owner labeling time nor detector evaluation. Any resumption requires
an explicit decision and the original blind-labeling safeguards.
